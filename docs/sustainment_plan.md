# Sustainment Plan

## Proposed handoff

Handoff owner: quality lead (fictional role placeholder; no person assigned). Before operationalization, a program sponsor must name an accountable owner and agree escalation coverage for each setting.

## Monitoring

Proposed cadence: weekly for eight weeks after a properly governed pilot, then monthly if stable. Review process, outcome, and balancing measures together; display denominators and missingness. Train each receiving role and check training coverage before handoff. Training coverage is not measured in this synthetic fixture.

## Reopen triggers

- Two consecutive weeks below 75% timely completion.
- Duplicate task rate above 10% for a review period.
- Material access barrier, missingness, or workflow burden signal.

Triggers are illustrative and unvalidated. They prompt a workgroup review, not automated care guidance or a conclusion about harm. Record the decision, owner, evidence, and follow-up date before closing a review.

## Control record

The demo project board stores a handoff role, cadence, and trigger so these are visible. It does not send reminders, measure staff training, establish actual ownership, or sustain a real process.
