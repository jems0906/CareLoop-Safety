# Measurement Plan

## Definitions frozen for the demonstration

These are portfolio definitions, not clinician-approved operating protocols.

| Measure | Type | Numerator | Denominator / unit | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Timely checklist completion | Process | Eligible transitions with completed checklist | All eligible transitions; percent | Within 48 hours of synthetic transition |
| Unresolved discrepancy | Outcome | Reviewed transitions with an unresolved discrepancy | All transitions reviewed; percent | Fixed 72-hour review checkpoint |
| Documentation time | Balancing | Total documented minutes | Mean minutes per transition | At checklist completion |
| Duplicate tasks | Balancing | Duplicate checklist tasks | Count per week | Weekly |
| Workflow burden | Balancing | Constructed staff burden rating | Synthetic 0-10 score; weekly mean | Weekly |

Eligibility is a synthetic transition intended to receive a checklist. Duplicate transitions are not represented in the fixture; a real data dictionary must specify deduplication and exclusions before analysis. Zero denominators yield a missing rate, never zero percent. Numerators above denominators are rejected.

## Equity and missingness

Setting-specific samples, access-barrier categories, and missing-record counts are fictional constructed values. Counts below 30 are suppressed as an illustrative small-cell rule, not a validated privacy or statistical threshold. Missing-record rate is missing eligible records / eligible transitions. Barriers are limited to handoff timing, document access, task duplication, and none recorded. These categories are not validated user-research findings, and small synthetic groups should not be read as disparity evidence. No disparity conclusion is supported.

## Analysis and limitations

Run charts show time order and mark the first constructed workflow test. The data are generated, not observed; before/after comparisons do not establish causality. No statistical process-control limits, risk adjustment, readmission measure, or harm outcome is claimed. Increased checklist completion does not establish reduced readmissions or prevented harm.

## Workflow burden evaluation proposal

Before a real pilot, agree a consented evaluation protocol for clicks per transition, duplicate entry/task rate, time to complete, and brief qualitative staff feedback. Compare measures by setting and access barrier only when definitions, minimum counts, privacy controls, and missingness are adequate. No such user research or time study has occurred in this portfolio.
